What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is when we teach computers to think, solve problems, or make decisions similar to how humans do. It allows machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Everyday Example
Smart speakers like Alexa or Siri understanding your voice commands to play music or set alarms.

What is ML?

Machine Learning (ML) is a specific type of AI. Instead of giving a computer exact, step-by-step instructions, we give it lots of data. The computer uses this data to find patterns and learn how to do a task on its own.

Everyday Example
Streaming services analyzing the shows you watch to recommend new movies you might like.

Where is AI used?

AI is everywhere in our modern world, helping to make systems faster, smarter, and more efficient across various industries.

  • Healthcare: Assisting doctors in diagnosing diseases.
  • Education: Providing personalized tutoring apps.
  • Transport: Powering self-driving car navigation.
  • Gaming: Creating realistic video game opponents.
